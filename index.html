Frequently asked questions

Are these live trending Reels?

No. This starter site includes evergreen example ideas. A real-time trends section needs a reliable data source and regular editorial updates.

Is ReelTrend free?

Yes. This static starter website and its browser-based tools can be hosted on a free static hosting plan.

Can I earn with ads?

Possibly, after publishing substantial original value and meeting the ad provider's eligibility and policy requirements. Approval and earnings are not guaranteed.

Does the generator use AI?

This demo uses simple templates in your browser. It does not call a paid AI API or send your topic to a server.
